## Observed gaps

- The explicit-column-break case is rejected by output verification: a source paragraph anchor has no measurable fragment in the paginated layout.
- The lower-Roman endnote case is rejected by the renderer's strict font policy. The production diagnostic reports a non-exact, unverified, or incompletely covered font outcome; this run does not establish a more specific root cause.
- The footnote PDF contains `LiberationSans` although the fixture requests only Liberation Serif. Visible text and bottom-note placement pass, but the font check forces the case score to zero. Visual review also shows a blue body note marker where the reference is black.
- Inherited table cell padding is visibly absent, making the table shorter and moving following text upward. The nested-table case instead leaves additional vertical space in its outer cell.
- Tight wrapping uses a rectangular text exclusion area rather than the diamond contour seen in the reference. The square-wrapped graphic is also horizontally displaced.
- Continuous columns split paragraph C10 across columns despite its keep-lines property; the reference moves that paragraph intact to the right column.

The page-start heading is the one close-match case. Balanced columns and the authored agreement retain the intended structure but still show pixel differences. Prioritize the rejected exports and note font inheritance, then inherited cell margins, contour wrapping, and column keep-lines behavior.
